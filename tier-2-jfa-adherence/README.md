# Tier 2: Janus Facing Architecture Adherence

*The goal of Tier 2 is to understand the JFA standard as a process.*

JFA is an evolving standard for producing and governing commons software. NTARI balances its duties to Kentucky and the IRS with its duties as steward of the commons. Conformance reports and binding-coverage studies belong here.
