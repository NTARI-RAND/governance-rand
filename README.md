# governance-rand

Research & Development on the Governance Layer of the Janus Facing Architecture.

Where and how humans assemble to act on the stack. Leaving is dear, so members vote here.

Research is discussed in #rand-governance and voted on in #federation-governance in the NTARI Slack workspace.

## Structure

| Tier | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Tier 1: Bylaws of the Network Theory Applied Research Institute](tier-1-bylaws/) | Understand the relationship of the organization to territorial authority |
| [Tier 2: Janus Facing Architecture Adherence](tier-2-jfa-adherence/) | Understand the JFA standard as a process |
| [Tier 3: The Governance Platform](tier-3-governance-platform/) | Implement the bylaws and the JFA standard in the governance layer code; Slack and its workflows stand in until the official system is built |

## How we research

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your file to the correct tier folder.
3. Create a pull request with a [DCO signature](https://github.com/apps/dco).
4. Wait for peer review.

#federation-governance has 90 days to deliberate your publication, during which you defend your claims and make the changes reviewers ask for. Follow the [Research & Development Submission Guide](https://ntari.slack.com/docs/T0978JCFFSL/F0BTB5N93A9): the method, the ethics check and the submission template.

Merged research is published in #federation-volunteers, on [www.ntari.org](https://www.ntari.org) and affiliated social media channels, with the author(s) credited.
